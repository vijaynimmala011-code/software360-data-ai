# License Renewal Policy

> **Fictional training document.** All organizations, people, rules,
> thresholds, dates, costs, contacts, and examples are invented for Software
> 360. This is not an operational company policy and contains no confidential
> information.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines a fictional review process for software license renewals,
right-sizing, renegotiation, and retirement.

## 2. Review timeline

The Software Owner begins review 90 days before the contract end date. Finance
and Security reviewers receive the evidence package at least 60 days before
renewal, and a decision is recorded 30 days before the end date.

## 3. Required evidence

The review includes annual cost, licensed seats, assigned users, active users,
utilization trend, support demand, and fictional security review status.

## 4. Decision outcomes

Allowed outcomes are renew, right-size, renegotiate, or retire. Each outcome
must identify an owner, effective date, and measurable follow-up action.

## 5. Review roles

The fictional Software Owner supplies usage context. The Finance Reviewer
checks cost assumptions. The Security Reviewer confirms that known risks and
exceptions are documented.

## 6. Exceptions and documentation

An exception records its rationale, approver role, expiration date, and review
condition. Exceptions do not bypass final access controls.
